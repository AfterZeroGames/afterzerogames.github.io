Layer Café: delete your data

By After Zero Studio · Updated November 6, 2026

No account needed. Layer Café has no sign-up, so there is no account to close.
Progress on your phone: uninstall the game or clear its data and it is gone at once.
Online data (duels, profile, contest entries, statistics): send us one email and we delete it within 30 days.

1. Delete what is on your phone

Your coins, beans, levels, café and settings are saved only on your phone. To delete them: open Android Settings › Apps › Layer Café › Storage › Clear data, or uninstall the game. This cannot be undone.

2. Ask us to delete your online data

  1. Note the names you used: your Google Play Games player name (shown in the game's rankings) and, if you played Coffee Duels, your barista name (shown on the 1v1 screen).
  2. Email afterzero.contact@gmail.com with the subject "Layer Café data deletion" and those names.
  3. We confirm by email and delete the data within 30 days.

What we delete

  • Your public player profile (name, café snapshot, stats, medals).
  • Your Coffee Duels results and recorded runs.
  • Your photo contest entries and votes.

What we keep, and for how long

  • Purchase records are kept by Google Play under its own policies; we can't delete them.
  • Leaderboard scores and achievements are stored in your Google Play Games profile; you can remove them in the Play Games app or your Google account.
  • Game statistics (Firebase Analytics) are not linked to your name, only to a random app-instance ID; clearing the game's data starts a new one, and old statistics are deleted automatically by Firebase.
  • Ad data is handled by Google AdMob; you can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings › Privacy › Ads.
  • Without a request, duel data is deleted after 12 months, profiles not updated for 12 months are deleted, and contest entries and votes are deleted within 12 months after the contest ends.
